### Algorithmic Growth Rules
1. **The 30-Minute Golden Window:** The X algorithm judges a post's lifetime reach in the first 30–45 minutes. Secure 3–5 comments or retweets from accounts with 10k–50k followers immediately upon publishing.
2. **High-Volume Cadence:** In the growth phase, post **2 to 4 times per day**. A single breakout post (200k–2.4M views) drives 80% of follower and conversion spikes.
3. **Visual Contrast:** 80%+ of mobile X users use Dark Mode. Screenshots with bright, clean light backgrounds or ultra-high contrast code themes stop thumbs mid-scroll.
4. **No Links in Hook Tweets:** Never put outbound external links in Tweet 1. Always place links in Tweet 4 or the first auto-reply to avoid algorithmic reach penalties.

## 2. Core Twitter Copywriting & Hook Principles

### A. Hook Fundamentals
- **Target the Reader Directly:** *"You can scan your repo for leaked keys in 3 seconds"* beats *"Developers are scanning repos..."*
- **Lead with Pain + Outcome First:** Put the catastrophic loss or massive win in line 1.
- **Avoid Fatigued Clichés:** Ban *"Most people don't know..."*, *"Everyone is talking about X..."*, or *"In today's fast-paced world..."*.
- **Specific Numbers > Vague Claims:** *"$10,400 OpenAI bill"* beats *"a huge surprise bill"*; *"60% of 50 apps"* beats *"many apps"*.

### B. Formatting for Skimmers
- **Strict 1–2 Sentence Paragraphs:** Never create a 3-line block of text.
- **Whitespace is Oxygen:** Double line-break between every key thought.
- **Action Verbs:** Use *built, scraped, leaked, audited, cracked, bypassed, deployed*.
- **Contractions:** Use *I'll, won't, can't, didn't* (academic formal English kills engagement).
- **The Out-Loud Test:** Read every draft aloud. If your tongue stumbles, rewrite it.

### C. Psychological Triggers
- **Catastrophic Blindspot:** People care 10x more about avoiding disaster (DB dumped on Product Hunt day) than gaining a marginal improvement.
- **Zero-Friction Reassurance:** *"100% offline, zero network requests, no OAuth, no email BS"*.
